# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and authors submit and track academic manuscripts. Lecturers review assigned submissions, and administrators manage the publication workflow.

## Product Purpose

HyperData Lab supports academic manuscript submission, review, revision, and publication. The product should help users complete their current research workflow clearly and reliably.

## Brand Personality

Professional, clear, trustworthy. The interface should support focused academic work.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy visuals, decorative dashboard patterns, heavy surfaces, and controls that look interactive but do not work.

## Design Principles

- Keep research content central to the experience.
- Make workflow state and available actions easy to understand.
- Preserve accurate document rendering and dependable interactions.
- Use the shared HyperData Lab visual system consistently.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard interaction, clearly visible focus, readable contrast, and responsive layouts. No additional accessibility requirements have been specified.
